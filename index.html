Amir Taherin

PhD Candidate, Electrical and Computer Engineering · Northeastern University

Email CV Google Scholar GitHub LinkedIn

I am a PhD candidate at Northeastern University's NUCAR Laboratory, advised by Professors David Kaeli and Yanzhi Wang. I work on efficient and dependable AI inference on edge hardware: measuring where large language models, vision, and robotic workloads spend their time and energy, and building runtime methods that adapt computation to conditions. I was selected for the 2026 Future Leaders of AI program at the ACM AI Leadership Summit, and I expect to complete my PhD in 2026.
